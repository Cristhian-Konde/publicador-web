Privacy Policy

OffRoad Biker Publicador · Last updated: October 20, 2026

OffRoad Biker Publicador is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is used by a single creator to upload their own videos to their own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

What data we access

  • Basic profile information of the creator's own account (display name), to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to the creator's own account.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the creator's computer, encrypted by the operating system's credential manager.
  • Videos are sent directly from the creator's computer to the platform.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data from any other users.

Deleting data

The creator can disconnect the account at any time from the app or from the platform's settings, which removes the stored access.
